Will I lose muscle? Honest answer: some lean mass can go along with weight loss.

In a body-scan substudy of 140 adults, fat mass fell about 19% and lean mass about 10%. Lean tissue's share of body mass still rose by 3 points, because fat fell more.

That is why a scan beats the weighing scale, and why protecting muscle belongs in the plan.

Source: STEP 1 DEXA substudy, ENDO 2021 (via Patient Care) and Wilding et al. abstract.
