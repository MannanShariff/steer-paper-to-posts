=== CAROUSEL ===
Your BMI says 'normal'. Your waist may disagree.

Studies find South Asians tend to carry more body fat at the same BMI than white Caucasians, and health risks can show up at lower BMI and waist measurements. That is why Asian Indian guidance draws the lines lower: BMI from 23 for overweight, and a waist of 80 cm (women) or 90 cm (men) as the flag for abdominal obesity.

These are screening flags, not a diagnosis. A doctor reads them alongside your labs.

Source: Nutrients 2013;5(7):2708, Obesity and Dyslipidemia in South Asians.
