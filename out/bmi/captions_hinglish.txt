Aapka BMI kehta hai 'normal'. Kamar shayad na kahe.

Studies batati hain ki South Asians mein same BMI par body fat zyada ho sakta hai. Isliye Asian Indian guidance mein lines neeche hain: overweight BMI 23 se, aur kamar 80 cm (mahila) ya 90 cm (purush).

Ye screening flags hain, diagnosis nahi. Doctor aapke labs ke saath padhte hain.
