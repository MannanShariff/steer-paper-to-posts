Weight regain and cardiometabolic effects after withdrawal of semaglutide: The STEP 1 trial extension

Source: Wilding et al., Diabetes Obes Metab 2022 · machine checks: 0 failures 0 notes

Message. Weight coming back after stopping is biology, not failure, so a good programme plans the 'after' from day one.
Why it works. 'Will I gain it all back?' is the most emotional doubt in the brief and the one competitors dodge. We state the trial number plainly, say what the data cannot tell you, then turn the fear into a question you can ask a doctor. That matches Steer's 'a planned exit' promise.
